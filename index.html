Our story

We are two people
and one stove.

It started with a winter that cracked one of us open, and a shelf of products that all promised the same thing and delivered none of it.

The answer turned out to be a recipe in the back of a household ledger. Oats, milk, oil, patience. We spent five years learning to make it properly, and then five more learning when to leave it alone.

Everything is poured, cut and wrapped by hand. That is not a marketing line, it is a ceiling. We can only make what four hands finish in a week, so that is what we make.
